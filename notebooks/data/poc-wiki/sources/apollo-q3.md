# Apollo launch postponed to Q3 2026

After surfacing two new severity-1 blockers in the integration
review on 2026-04-21, project Apollo's launch has been postponed
from Q2 to Q3 2026.

New launch date: 2026-08-15. Decision logged by Priya; cross-team
notice sent to engineering, product, and ops on 2026-04-22.

The original Q2 plan is no longer valid.
