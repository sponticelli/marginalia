# Postpone Apollo to Q3 2026

Apollo's launch is **postponed from Q2 to Q3 2026**. New target ship
date: **2026-08-15**. This decision was logged on 2026-04-22 by the
project owner; cross-team notice was sent to engineering, product, and
ops the same day. Source of record: [[sources/apollo-q3]] and the
mid-cycle blocker review in
[[sources/apollo-launch-blockers-sync]].

## Rationale

- Two new severity-1 blockers were surfaced in the integration review
  on 2026-04-21.
- The original Q2 plan is no longer valid.

This postponement supersedes any earlier Q2 commitment. The wiki
should reflect Q3 as the current launch quarter.
